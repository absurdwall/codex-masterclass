Topic 04 · Core session · 8 min

Use dots & Space

Move from a conversation to ongoing work and a useful shared record.

In the sessionDetailed reading guide

Four roles, one workflow

A dot is an always-on agent in ChatGPT. Space is where editable Pages, files, and shared work live. Use them together when the work needs follow-through and a result people can return to.

UseWhen you need…Try asking…
A chatDiscussion or an immediate task“Compare these two proposals.”
A dotProgress on an ongoing responsibility“Help me prepare the workshop. Bring me unresolved decisions.”
A saved scheduleWork at an explicit time or supported event“Check the specified sources every Monday for four weeks.”
A Page in SpaceAn editable, reusable record“Save the recommendation, evidence, and open questions.”

These roles overlap. A dot can manage scheduled work and edit a Page; a normal chat can also perform multi-step tasks.

Teaching example · workshop planning
  1. Delegate a result

    Provide three venue proposals. Ask for total costs, trade-offs, source links, and unknowns. Keep contacting venues outside the assignment.

  2. Review and save

    Check the recommendation, then save it in a Workshop Page. Keep the decision and the detailed comparison easy to find.

  3. Collaborate on the artifact

    Ask your dot in a Page comment to resolve one discrepancy. Review the changed section, then share with the intended reviewer.

For recurring work, set up and verify a schedule separately. Writing “update every Monday” on a Page does not establish an enabled task.

Work with agents in Space

Three checks before you delegate

Where will it run? Cloud work can continue with your laptop off. Steps using that laptop need it online.

What may it do? A request to draft is not permission to send. State the review point.

Who can see the result? A shared Page exposes its content and child pages. External sources retain their own permissions.
