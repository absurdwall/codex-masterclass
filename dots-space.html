01 / The assistant

What is a dot?

A dot is your ongoing personal AI assistant in ChatGPT. You talk to it, give it work, and come back to the results.

It can research information, make documents, build software, and coordinate tasks using the tools and sources it can access. You can keep talking to it while work is underway, or return later.

Watch OpenAI’s dots introduction · 2:26A short product film on OpenAI’s site, rather than a setup tutorial.

For example

“Help me plan our workshop.”

Give your dot the brief and venue proposals. It can compare the options, prepare a recommendation, and bring you the questions that need a decision.

You get: completed research and a next decision, rather than having to direct every individual step.

What makes it useful?

  • It can do work between conversations. Suitable tasks run on its cloud computer, even while your laptop is off
  • It can use relevant context. With the right connections, it can work with your apps, files, preferences, and ongoing projects
  • You can steer it as you go. Add a constraint, change the priority, or review a result before the next action

A normal chat can also use tools and solve multi-step problems. The point of a dot is having an ongoing assistant to return to, with work and follow-through across conversations.

02 / The documents

What is ChatGPT Space?

Space is where you create, organize, and share editable Pages and files in ChatGPT.

A Page is a document you can keep working on: a plan, a comparison, meeting notes, or a guide. A space groups related Pages and files around a project or topic.

For example

A Workshop space

Keep the agenda, venue comparison, and planning notes together. Open the current plan, edit it yourself or ask an agent to change it, and invite a colleague to review it.

You get: a document people can find, edit, and revisit, instead of an answer buried in a conversation.

What can you do in it?

  • Create a useful document. Start a Page or turn work from a conversation into one; combine text, tables, images, and supporting files
  • Work with an agent on the Page. Ask for a revision, or mention your dot in a comment to request a focused update
  • Collaborate with people. Share with the appropriate available view, comment, or edit access

Space is the place for the material, not an assistant doing the work. Saving a Page does not automatically keep it updated.
