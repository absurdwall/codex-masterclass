Before we start

Open your Codex / ChatGPT desktop app and check the models and tools available to your account. Keep a small, non-sensitive task in mind. You can follow the explanations even if a live-demo feature is unavailable.

A useful habit for every topic

1

Define the result

Say what should exist when the task is done. Include the audience, constraints, and the files it can use.

2

Choose the setup

Pick a model, enough thinking effort, and a workspace with the right context and permissions.

3

Verify before trusting

Open the output, test the important path, and ask for evidence when a result matters.
