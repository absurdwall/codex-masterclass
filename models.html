Choose a model

Balance capability, thinking effort, and cost against the work in front of you.

Three controls: model, effort, and speed

Model = the engine

The model determines the underlying capabilities. A faster, cheaper model may be enough for a narrow edit; a more capable model may help with ambiguous problems.

Effort = room to think

Reasoning effort controls how much reasoning the model can use. Higher effort can improve difficult answers, but usually costs time and tokens. It is not a guarantee of correctness.

Speed = a service tier, not extra intelligence

Fast is a separate control from model and reasoning effort. Where available, Fast supports Luna, Sol, and Astra. It uses 2.5× included subscription usage, or 2× purchased credits / pay-as-you-go usage, versus Standard. Those are usage multipliers, not a promise of 2× or 2.5× speed. It does not guarantee faster tools or image generation.

Speed guide
Model ≠ product

Codex and Claude Code are agent products. GPT and Claude are model families. Product tools, prompts, context, permissions, and model routing all affect the result. An API benchmark is not an end-to-end product ranking.

Three GPT starting points for this class

The official model-selection guide informs the task roles below. These starting presets are our course judgment; the broad intelligence score is not a coding ranking.

Default: Sol Medium

Scoped features and everyday work. GPT-6.1 Sol medium scores 48 at $0.21 per benchmark task. Give it relevant files and a clear definition of done before adding more reasoning.

Escalate: Sol Xhigh

Difficult bugs and verification. Sol xhigh reaches 51 at $0.39. Use it when medium misses a specific edge case. It is a smaller cost step than jumping straight to Astra.

Quality-first: Astra Medium

Broad context, ambiguity, and multi-step work. Official guidance positions Astra for demanding, multi-step work; Medium is our suggested quality-first starting point. Consider Max when extra depth matters more than time or usage, or as another attempt on a hard issue.

Where Luna fits

Luna max is the low-cost comparison: 37 at $0.07. Use small, easy-to-review tasks to see whether its quality is enough. For the pet demo, Luna Medium remains a suggested rehearsal setting, not a proven optimum.

Claude, Gemini, and Grok remain useful comparison points. The presets above are GPT-only because this is a Codex class; the benchmark is not a ranking of complete agent products.

What can a $20 or $100 subscription be worth in API spend?

For heavy use, potentially much more than the monthly fee. These September 2026 reports give a rough sense of scale, based on estimated API-equivalent tokens and full allowance use.

How these rough ranges were constructed

Plus: one user’s short-context allowance tests projected $167–$283 over 30 days across models. A separate single-user study reported $61–$81 per full week in its recent seven windows, roughly $261–$347 when normalized to 30 days. Rounded together: $170–$350.

Pro100: one user estimated about $200 in a capped week ($800 across four such weeks); another estimated about $2,000 per month from their own token/cost tool. This is a rough illustration from two reports, not a statistical range.

These are historical API-equivalent estimates, not API credits or guaranteed current limits. Pro reports predate late-September policy changes; Plus evidence straddles that period. Four-week and 30-day projections are approximate. Check current plan usage rules before buying.
